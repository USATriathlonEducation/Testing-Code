7-Zone Cycling Training Intensity Guide

Zone structure: Zones 1–2 are below LT1 (aerobic base). Zones 3–4 span LT1 to LT2 (threshold development). Zones 5–7 are above LT2 (high-intensity and neuromuscular power). Percent of LT2 / FTP, power, heart rate, watts/kg, and RPE ranges are shown for each zone. The percentage column uses whole numbers with no overlap between zones so it can be entered straight into TrainingPeaks.
